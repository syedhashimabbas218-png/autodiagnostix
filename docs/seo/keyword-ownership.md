## Operating rules

- No changes to titles, primary keyword targeting or indexation were implemented from this matrix.
- No city-page proliferation. Only propose a location page if genuine distinct service information exists.
- Strong existing AutoDiagnostix generic landing pages keep their roles; category pages remain browseable product collections. Query/page overlap is an investigation signal, not proof of harm.
- Preserve LAUNCH product URLs on both domains. No cross-domain canonical, deindexation, redirect, copied FAQs or sitewide keyword links.
- Before medium-risk targeting changes, obtain sister-site GSC query/page exports for the same dates, segment branded/model/generic intent, compare landing pages and document likely impact on both businesses.
- Missing brands, categories and questions are opportunities to verify, not instructions to fabricate catalog items or produce bulk generic articles.
